  <files>src/animation/SpriteEffects.ts, src/config/constants.ts</files>
  <action>
    Create `src/animation/SpriteEffects.ts` with two exported functions:

    1. `invertSprite(base64: string): Promise<string>` -- Takes a raw base64 PNG string (no `data:` prefix). Creates an Image, draws it to a temporary Canvas, gets ImageData, inverts RGB channels (255 - value) while preserving alpha, converts back to base64 via `toDataURL('image/png')`, and strips the `data:image/png;base64,` prefix before returning. This produces the "flash" inversion effect for damage hits.

    2. `blankSprite(): string` -- Creates a 1x1 transparent PNG via Canvas and returns its raw base64 string. Used to "hide" a sprite container without removing it (for blank flash frames and faint removal).
