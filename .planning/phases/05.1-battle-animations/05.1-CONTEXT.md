# Phase 5.1: Battle Animations - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-02-27
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Transform the static turn result display into a Game Boy-style animated sequence. After the player selects an action, turn events play out one-by-one with typewriter text, sprite flash effects, and progressive HP drain — all concurrent. Sprites remain visible throughout. This phase modifies the turn resolution flow in ScreenController and adds an animation engine. No new screens or game mechanics.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Text streaming feel
- Per-character typewriter reveal (~40-60ms per character), not per-word
- Punctuation gets extra pause (~150-200ms on '!', '?', '.', '...')  — adds dramatic rhythm
- Multiple log entries auto-advance with ~500ms pause between entries (no tap needed between)
- Sprites stay visible during text streaming (Game Boy-style layout, not text-only TURN_RESULT)
- Text streams in the text area of the battle HUD layout while sprites remain in place

### Sprite hit reactions
- Damage hit: both invert flash AND blank flash combined (invert→restore→blank→restore = 1 cycle)
- 2 flash cycles per hit (~400-600ms total, 4 image updates)
- Miss: attacker sprite does one brief flash (disappear/reappear) to suggest an attempt; defender stays still
- Faint: 2-3 rapid flashes, then sprite removed from display entirely
- Sprite animations happen CONCURRENTLY with text streaming and HP drain (not sequentially)

### HP drain pacing
- Frame-by-frame drain: HP ticks down one block at a time via rapid updateText() calls
- Both the visual bar [████░░] AND the percentage number update together in sync
- Uniform drain speed (fast and smooth) — no dramatic slowdown at low HP
- HP drain starts DURING the sprite flash (concurrent), not after it finishes

### Playback control
- Tap during animation = skip to end of ALL entries instantly (final state shown immediately)
- All gestures except tap are blocked during animation (scrolls, double-tap ignored)
- After animation sequence finishes: auto-transition to next screen (BATTLE_HUD, SWITCH_SELECT, or END_SCREEN) — no "tap to continue"
- ~500ms pause between log entries during auto-advance

### Claude's Discretion
- Exact ms per character for typewriter (target ~40-60ms, tune for G2 BLE responsiveness)
- How many HP drain steps (balance smoothness vs update count)
- Whether to pre-compute inverted sprites at cache time or generate on-the-fly
- Transition timing between animation end and next screen
- How to handle the text area layout within the existing battle HUD container system

</decisions>
