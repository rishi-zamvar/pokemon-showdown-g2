### Human Verification Required

The following behaviors are structurally complete but require physical G2 hardware or the simulator to confirm the visual/timing experience:

#### 1. Typewriter text visual appearance on G2 display

**Test:** Start a battle, select an attack, observe the glass display during turn resolution.
**Expected:** Text for each turn event appears character-by-character at a readable pace (~45ms/char), with longer pauses at sentence-ending punctuation.
**Why human:** `updateText` drives the SDK's `textContainerUpgrade` call — whether the SDK actually renders each incremental update visibly on the G2 waveguide display (vs. batching/dropping frames) requires hardware observation.

#### 2. Sprite flash visual quality on G2 display

**Test:** Trigger a damage event and watch the sprite container on the glasses.
**Expected:** The opponent sprite briefly flickers (inverted colors, then blank, then restored) before HP updates — should feel like a Game Boy damage flash.
**Why human:** Canvas pixel inversion is correct in code, but the `updateImageRawData` SDK call frequency (4 frames at 75ms each) on real hardware may appear smoother or more jarring than expected.

#### 3. HP drain visual smoothness

**Test:** Observe the HP bar text updating during a damage animation.
**Expected:** The `[████░░░░░░] 45%` bar decrements visibly step-by-step before reaching the final value.
**Why human:** `updateText` is called once per HP step — whether the G2 firmware renders these incremental text updates at 40ms intervals or batches them needs hardware confirmation.

#### 4. Tap-to-skip responsiveness feel

**Test:** Tap the glasses during a multi-entry turn animation.
**Expected:** The display immediately jumps to the final battle state without playing out remaining events.
**Why human:** The skip logic is structurally correct, but the perceived latency depends on how quickly pending `await glass.updateImage` or `await glass.updateText` SDK calls complete after cancellation is set — SDK call duration is opaque.
